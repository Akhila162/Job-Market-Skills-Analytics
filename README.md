# Job Market & Skills Demand Analytics

## Project Overview

**Job Market & Skills Demand Analytics** is a data analytics project that explores job postings in the Indian job market to understand hiring trends, in-demand skills, salary ranges, and experience requirements.

The project uses SQL, Python, and LibreOffice Calc to clean, analyze, visualize, and present job market data.

## Objectives

* Identify the most frequently posted job roles.
* Analyze job locations and hiring distribution.
* Find the most demanded technical and CSE/IT skills.
* Explore salary ranges across job postings.
* Understand experience requirements.
* Compare salary ranges with required experience.

## Dataset

The project uses an Indian Job Market dataset containing **97,929 job postings**.

Key columns include:

* Job title
* Company name
* Skills and tags
* Job location
* Salary range
* Minimum and maximum experience
* Job description
* Company reviews and ratings

## Tools & Technologies

* **MySQL** – Data storage and SQL analysis

* **Jupyter Notebook** – Interactive analysis

* **GitHub** – Project documentation and version control

## Dashboard

The dashboard presents visual summaries of job roles, locations, skills, salary ranges, and experience requirements.

## Project Outcomes

This project helps explore:

* Which job roles appear most frequently in the dataset.
* Which skills are commonly mentioned in job postings.
* How salary ranges vary across postings.
* What experience levels employers request.
* Which locations have higher numbers of postings.

## Limitations

* The dataset represents the available job postings and may not reflect the entire Indian job market.
* Some salary information may be missing or undisclosed.
* Job upload dates may be relative text rather than exact dates.
* Skill demand is based on the skills listed in the dataset.
